Reading: with the shipped weights the crude player loses two matches in three, and few matches
end before turn 30 (27 of 100). Raising the danger weight makes the AI stall (15 draws), lowering it
makes the AI easier. **No weight was changed**: GDD 9 states no win-rate target, so any value would be a
guess. To decide: a target win rate for a first-time player (suggested 40 to 60 % against this
bot, more against a good player), then a sweep of `w_danger`, `w_damage`, `leash`, the starting
units and `energie.json.start` against it.
