# Balance run of 2026-11-02 (AI against a scripted player)

Tool: `tests/balance/run_matches.gd`
(`godot --headless -s tests/balance/run_matches.gd -- <first seed> <count> [map=marais] [file.json.key=value ...]`).
The player is `ScriptedPlayer`: it upgrades, captures, strikes what it reaches and walks toward the
nearest node, ruin or enemy. It is a **crude** player, a floor for a human, not a measure of one.
No refused command in any match.

**Target (proposed, to confirm by the game-designer)**: the crude player wins 40 to 60 % of its
matches on every map; a human, who plays better, wins more.

## First measurement (starting units as designed, 100 seeds, plaine)

| Variant | Player wins | Player loses | Draws |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shipped data | 33 | 67 | 0 |
| `w_danger` 0 (AI ignores exposure) | 43 | 57 | 0 |
| `w_danger` 12 | 33 | 52 | 15 |
| `w_danger` 3, 2, 1 / `w_damage` 7 / `w_lethal` 15 / `w_node` 4 / `leash` 3 | 28 to 33 | 67 to 72 | 0 |
| `leash` 2 | 63 | 22 | 15 |
| `w_danger` 3, `w_node` 6, `w_progress` 1 | 100 | 0 | 0 |

The AI weights act by steps, not smoothly, and two of them make the AI stall (draws) or give the match away.
**No AI weight was changed**: the weights stay the GDD 9 values. The starting units (provisional, GDD 6 open
question) were the lever instead.

## Decision: starting units

| Change | Plaine (seeds 1-100) | Marais (seeds 1-100) |
|---|---|---|
| none | 33 % | 85 % (the second map was far too easy for the player) |
| plaine: + 1 rempart and + 1 eclaireur for the pack | **58 %** (12 eliminated, 30 lost on turns) | |
| marais: + 3 chasseurs for the Guardians | | **65 %** (35 lost on turns) |

Other starting-unit variants tried on plaine: + rempart alone 28 % (59 draws), + assaut alone 83 %,
+ ouvrier alone 34 %. On marais: + 1 chasseur 85 %, + 2 chasseurs 69 %, + 2 chasseurs and a bastion 69 %.

Final check, shipped data, two series of 100 seeds:

| Map | Seeds 1-100 | Seeds 101-200 | Mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| plaine | 58 % | 61 % | 59.5 % |
| marais | 65 % | 49 % | 57 % |

Reading: both maps sit near the top of the target for a crude player; the series differ by up to 16 points,
so a hundred seeds give a band of about ±8 points. Few matches end before turn 30 on marais (the map is
larger and the units farther apart). To refine with human players.
